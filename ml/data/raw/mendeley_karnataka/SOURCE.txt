4. CLINICAL CAPTURE DETAILS & GROUND TRUTH
------------------------------------------
Capture Hardware:
  - Standard consumer mobile phone cameras
  - Intraoral clinical cameras
Clinical Environment:
  - Hospital dental clinics and outpatient oncology examination rooms
Diagnostic Ground Truth:
  - Clinically diagnosed and/or histopathologically confirmed oral lesions
  - Categorized by dental and oncological specialists into benign versus malignant oral cavity lesions (predominantly oral squamous cell carcinoma and oral potentially malignant disorders)

6. ONCOLINK USAGE & CLINICAL NOTES
----------------------------------
- Per ONCOLINK SPEC Section 6, the model baseline (EfficientNet-B0) is trained on patient-split clinical photographs.
- Data augmentation applied in `augmented_data` is offline reference; the ONCOLINK ML pipeline should prioritize original data (`original_data/`) and control its own patient-level splits and cross-validation to prevent data leakage.
- Intended Use Notice: Prototype research and triage development only. Public clinical-photo datasets are small and not representative of all skin tones, mobile sensors, and lighting conditions.
