4. CLINICAL CAPTURE DETAILS & GROUND TRUTH
------------------------------------------
Capture Hardware:
  - Consumer digital cameras, mobile smartphones, and clinical documentation photography
Anatomical Sites:
  - Lips (upper and lower labial mucosa, vermilion border)
  - Tongue (dorsal, lateral borders, ventral surface)
Diagnostic Ground Truth:
  - Binary classification:
    * `cancer`: Oral squamous cell carcinoma / biopsy-confirmed malignancy
    * `non-cancer`: Normal oral mucosa, benign aphthous ulcers, traumatic keratosis, benign tongue conditions

6. ONCOLINK USAGE & CLINICAL NOTES
----------------------------------
- Per ONCOLINK SPEC Section 6, this dataset serves as an outside-image benchmark / validation cohort to test model generalization across distinct cameras, illumination setups, and clinical sites.
- SPEC Section 6 Requirement: Report internal test results and outside-image results separately, as performance on outside image distributions is typically lower.
- Class Imbalance: 87 cancer vs 44 non-cancer. Metric evaluation should focus on Sensitivity / Recall, Specificity, AUROC, and class-balanced thresholds.
- Intended Use Notice: Prototype research and triage development only. Not approved for autonomous clinical diagnosis.
